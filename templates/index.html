Workflow Architecture

Why Complaints Can't Be Closed Without You

Traditional portals let officers unilaterally close tickets. In this Sachivalayam system, true transparency is guaranteed:

1

Citizen Lodges Request

Citizen files grievance or applies for certificate renewal with details & ward location.

2

Officer Assigned

Approved Sachivalayam Officer picks up request, inspects site, and moves to In Progress.

3

Officer Submits Work

Officer marks work done. Status moves to Pending Citizen Confirmation.

4

Citizen Confirms "DONE"

Citizen checks site/certificate. If satisfied, clicks Accept → officially sealed as DONE!
